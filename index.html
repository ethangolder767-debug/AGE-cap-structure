Capital structure for Campus Deli and Sub Shop

Every number below is worked out in your browser from the CSV files in this app's data/ folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Question c: Firm U and Firm L

Two firms with the same assets and the same EBIT in each kind of year. Firm L borrows; Firm U doesn't. ROE uses book equity (assets − debt). TIE is EBIT ÷ interest, so it is n/a for a firm with no interest.

Question d: CDSS at each amount of borrowing

Earnings at each amount of debt

EBIT stays the same at every amount of debt. Interest = rd × debt. Net income = (EBIT − interest) × (1 − T). CDSS pays out all its earnings, so dividends per share = EPS and g = 0.

Case method: buy back at today's $20 (questions d(3) and d(4))

Shares repurchased = debt ÷ $20. EPS = net income ÷ shares remaining. Price = EPS ÷ rs. WACC uses book weights: debt ÷ total assets and equity ÷ total assets.

New-price method: buy back at the price after the announcement

Equity value S = net income ÷ rs. Firm value V = S + debt. Price = V ÷ 100,000 shares, and the buyback happens at that price. WACC uses market weights: debt ÷ V and S ÷ V.
